# Local and expert research bridge

Use for a verified local market, specialist industry report, local panel or an irreducible offline/expert evidence gap. Never infer geography solely from Korean wording. Existing `KOREA_LOCAL_DISTRIBUTION.md`, `PRIMARY_RESEARCH_PLATFORM_BRIDGE.md`, sampling and ethics rules remain governing.

## Local evidence before imported proxies

For Korea, search relevant official statistics and disclosures first when they answer the question: KOSIS, the responsible ministry or regulator, DART, public trade bodies and accessible industry reports. These are source candidates, not mandatory reads. Preserve population, industry code/category, geography, period, unit and definition. A global statistic or another jurisdiction's panel is a proxy with limits, not a local market estimate.

Compare conflicting reports in a definition table before averaging: included products, revenue versus transactions, nominal/real currency, base year, observed/estimated/forecast, publication date and methodology. Reportlinker, Statista or specialist repositories can locate reports; a catalog listing or inaccessible paid report does not supply its underlying findings. Citation permission is not reproduction or commercial redistribution permission.

## Match fieldwork infrastructure to the evidence gap

For OpenSurvey, Pickply, a verified local provider, Ipsos Digital or another specialist, check target eligibility, recruitment source, geographic reach, incentives, quality exclusions, achievable composition, fieldwork mode and respondent burden. Name the instrument, data/export ownership, privacy and deliverable acceptance conditions. Panel size and quotas do not establish probability sampling or representativeness.

If a supplied name such as a transliterated local provider cannot be identified, retain the original name and source locator as unresolved. Do not invent its URL or infer capabilities from a similar company. Continue with another verified route when useful.

Separate survey-response collection from recruiting people for an interview or usability session. The handoff tracks invited, screened eligible, participated, completed and valid records, not a single 'recruited' count. Compare planned versus achieved composition; anticipated incidence is not measured incidence. Freeze instrument/quotas before fieldwork and log deviations. Synthetic personas remain synthetic even when a panel company supplies them.

## Expert/offline handoff that removes work rather than adding homework

For participant observation or ethnography, conditionally read the corresponding M013 or M014 row in `references/modules/ADVANCED_METHOD_HANDOFF_MATRIX.md`. Actual field access and a qualified human role remain distinct from an AI-prepared research brief.

First exhaust adequate public and already-provided evidence. If an unresolved question truly needs an expert or field visit, prepare the brief: decision, exact missing claim, why desk research cannot close it, target expertise/population, neutral questions or observation scenario, recruitment criteria, instrument, consent/privacy constraints, analysis and acceptance criteria.

Give the best bounded conclusion now. Make new interviews, panels, visits or purchases an optional escalation with cost and burden visible. No universal sample target, mandatory fixed-size focus group or universal 'three of five said yes' decision rule. Sample/design follow the claim and risk.

When field data return, check eligibility, missingness, deviations and provenance before synthesis. Expert opinion is expertise-linked testimony, not observed customer demand. Do not fabricate interviews, store visits, panel responses or expert endorsements.

## Output

Deliver the actual local evidence table or a ready-to-use research brief and explicit access gap. A recommendation to use a service is not a completed integration or completed fieldwork. Preserve translations next to critical source terms, units and original locators so another worker can recheck without repeating the search.
