# Search and commerce evidence

Load when search demand, competition changes, seller exports, product listings or customer-service patterns can change the decision. Use the applicable SECONDARY_DESK or COMPETITOR_ALTERNATIVE card in `references/methods/secondary-and-competitors.md`. Only load `RESEARCH_REFRESH_AND_CHANGE_IMPACT.md` when the user requests an update to a prior FieldPilot research baseline. A first analysis or an ordinary webpage snapshot comparison does not require that baseline-update module. This is not permission to run a persistent crawler or change a store.

## Search intent and paths

Group queries by the job behind them: problem discovery, category learning, alternatives, purchase intent, use/support or unrelated ambiguity. Preserve exact query, geography, language, device if relevant, time period, source and unit. Separate branded and nonbranded searches. Deduplicate variants without losing distinct intents.

Search volume, relative trend index, search impressions, clicks, unique visitors and customers are different quantities. A million searches does not mean a million buyers. Social mentions and keyword similarity do not prove an observed customer journey. ListeningMind or equivalent path data may support a sequence only for its stated coverage and methodology. Without actual path data, label a proposed journey as a hypothesis.

For keyword or traffic providers, check modeled versus observed data, coverage, freshness and export limitations. Use public search results and official documentation as fallback, while explicitly lowering claims that require proprietary clickstream or paid databases.

Keep observed query relationships, algorithmic clusters and interpreted intentions in separate fields. Record seed, direction and sorting/filter settings when interpreting a path tool. A search-engine relationship graph is not necessarily individual-level clickstream, even when the product calls it a path.

## Competitor change analysis

Save source URL, observed time, affected product/plan/geography, quoted or summarized fact and prior snapshot. Diff meaning: price, included limits, audience, distribution or claim, not page layout. A changed webpage is not proof customers adopted the change. Distinguish new observation from a change that occurred on a verified date.

Deliver a short battlecard when useful: customer job, relevant alternative, verified difference, trade-off, evidence link, unknown and what the user should change or keep. No invented win/loss rates. Refresh only affected decisions. Continuous monitoring requires an actual authorized scheduler and a change log, not a promise that a skill runs while idle.

## Seller and customer-service evidence

Given authorized exports, reconcile orders, cancellations, refunds, units, gross/net sales, fees and unit costs before drawing profitability conclusions. Preserve currency, tax treatment, period and channel. Do not equate third-party estimated marketplace sales with the seller's actual revenue.

Compare listings on total payable price, delivery, variant, bundle and conditions, not headline prices alone. Evaluate titles and content against real customer questions and platform rules; keyword density or a vendor score is not a sales guarantee.

For customer-service records, retain a redacted record ID and channel, classify issue and resolution, check a sample of AI labels, separate recurring users from repeated tickets, and connect themes to actual product or process changes. Report the sample base and unresolved cases. Sentiment cannot replace resolution rate or payment evidence.

Connect before/after listing or channel changes to a dated change log and exposure/measurement conditions. Seasonality, promotions, selection and product changes are alternative explanations. Without a comparison design, describe association rather than claim causal uplift.

## Deliverable

Only the task-relevant outputs are required: intent/source table, competitor change log, comparable offer table, reconciled seller table or coded customer-service evidence. Include decision implication and a scoped next action. Do not alter pricing, send promotions, connect stores or upload customer records without authority.
