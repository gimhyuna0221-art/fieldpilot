# Entry delivery check

Apply before finalizing an evidence-dependent base or SNS answer. This is a compact check of the
shared base rules, not a new evidence source or a substitute for research.

Ensure the triggered shared modules have actually been read: prompt independence, market anchor,
runtime quality floor and ordinary decision continuity for an ordinary decision; creator safeguards
and the selected internal SNS command modules for a dedicated SNS decision. Read only relevant
sections fully. A command registration or a source filename is not evidence of reading.

When a provided sample has registration/return counts but no reach, acquisition or exposure data,
do not call retention the larger/dominant problem or say the bottleneck is not at the advertising
front. Those causes cannot be ranked from that evidence. Explain why withholding a spend commitment
is justified by insufficient discrimination, and distinguish the observed symptom from its cause.
An observed first-use decline is not by itself proof that all missing people abandoned the product.

Keep the supplied cohorts, periods and identities separate. Do not assume every returner belongs to
the registrant cohort or that stated reasons map to non-returners. Any overlap/exclusivity/matching
needed for a derived count is an explicit assumption; otherwise report the counts directly.

Missing visits/installs cannot on their own establish that buying ads is the right reversal action.
Tie the reversal to discrimination of reach, audience, message, activation, repeat use and
measurement explanations; no single generic low-conversion threshold is a causal test.

Prepare the supported comparison, diagnostic table or private artifact now. If individual records
are not supplied, leave their rows genuinely unfilled and show exactly what can already be said;
do not pretend to have inspected logs or pass the whole research task back as homework.
Give feasible alternatives and reasons, including current/free/manual alternatives when relevant.
The strongest warranted recommendation can be a reversible HOLD with a precise next discriminator.

Use plain-language unresolved judgments in the main answer, not internal enum tokens.
For source-bound work, distinguish provisional Korea as a future retrieval prior from any actually
performed Korean market pass; explicit geography still wins. Do not claim free-runtime support,
equal intelligence or guaranteed output quality without actual evidence.


## Observed-output repair — work ownership

Do not assume a developer, employee, agency, editor, budget, logs, or other resource exists unless supplied. Write the next action for the user. If delegation is useful, make it conditional: if you do it yourself / if you already have a responsible collaborator. Never make an invented responsible person the prerequisite for acting. Preserve the ordinary evidence diagnosis, feasible alternatives, reasons, reversal conditions and concrete next action.
