# Creator concept start — creator-start-01

## Activation and precedence

Use only for starting or choosing an SNS/content channel concept, including “SNS 해보고 싶은데
관심사도 잘 모르겠고 경험도 없어”. Do not activate merely because someone is a beginner, asks in
casual language, or asks where to promote an existing product. Existing product-marketing cases keep
their diagnosis and `FREE_FIRST_AND_CHANNEL_ROUTING.md` route. An explicit narrow request such as
“채널 이름만” stays narrow and does not start intake or broader research.

This module supplies a small optional entry into the existing market-decision workflow. It does not
replace `PROMPT_SKILL_INDEPENDENCE.md` QUESTION_GATE, `MARKET_ANCHOR_AND_EXPANSION.md`,
`RUNTIME_QUALITY_FLOOR.md`, quality P0/P1 rules, professional source brokerage, evidence triage,
marketing judgment or `MARKET_DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.md`. No new crawler, paid tool, personality test,
success score, automatic posting, media production or PPT generation is added.

## Small question flow — only personal facts the user owns

Restore supplied interests, everyday experience, accessible materials and constraints first. Lack of
formal expertise is not lack of lived experience; do not fabricate qualifications or assume a novice
has no useful perspective. Public audience needs, competing channels and platform facts are FINDABLE:
research them, never ask the user to supply a competitor list or prove a market.

If the subject is too unidentified for a useful bounded investigation, or the user explicitly asks for
questions first, ASK_FIRST permits at most two questions in one message. Explain briefly that the
answers choose what to research and what the user can sustain. Example questions, adapted only for
missing decision-material facts:

1. “최근 자주 찾아보거나 직접 해본 일 중 하나가 있나요? 요리·게임·일상 정리처럼 작아도
   돼요. 없거나 모르겠으면 ‘몰라’라고 해도 돼요.”
2. “어느 정도로 만들 수 있나요? 예: 주 1시간, 얼굴 없이 글이나 화면으로, 추가 비용 없이.
   정해진 게 없으면 ‘몰라’라고 해도 돼요.”

These are examples, not a required questionnaire. When a subject is identifiable (for example
“자취 요리를 해보고 싶어”), research first and ASK_AFTER only for up to two remaining personal facts
that can reverse the recommendation. Never drip-feed a questionnaire over several turns. A response
of “몰라 / 없음 / 정해진 것 없음” is not asked again: use UNKNOWN, offer labeled reversible options,
and state how the unknown limits the decision. Do not require a country, platform choice, target
customer definition, model/subscription name or expert terminology before helping.

With no subject even after “몰라”, do not invent researched niche winners. Offer a bounded exploration
action: show a few plainly hypothetical formats tied to everyday observation (a process note, a
comparison, a beginner learning log) so the user can react, without claiming demand or expertise.
Any selection is provisional; research its actual audience and alternatives before recommending
investment. A hypothetical example is not a disguised market finding.

## Research before recommending a channel concept

For an identifiable subject, apply the existing local-first → global-best → local-transfer sequence.
Korean alone permits a provisional South Korea search pass, never confirmed geography. Do not replace
that pass with a country question. Discover broadly and inspect only decision-material candidates
deeply under `SOURCE_DISCOVERY_AND_SELECTION.md`; do not impose a fixed channel/source/search quota.

Connect the following inside the existing decision surface, rather than producing a second report:

- Audience/job and demand signal: what people seek or struggle with, the actual source and date/scope,
  and what the observation can establish. Separate recurring questions, search behavior, viewing,
  subscription/return behavior and observed payment. Search interest, views, likes or a popular
  competitor are not proof of demand for this new channel or payment/WTP.
- Actual competing channels: inspect relevant public channel/profile and representative content
  where accessible. Give real names, useful locators, inspected scope/date, audience promise,
  format/topic overlap, and the material difference. Include direct and substitute formats when they
  can change the decision; popularity alone does not select the best reference. Never invent a
  channel, unseen content, audience demographics, private analytics or current performance.
- Feasible distinction: link the proposed audience promise or format to specific inspected evidence
  and the user's actual ability/access. A missing feature or topic in sampled content is not a
  confirmed market gap. “초보자의 시선” or “AI로 만든 콘텐츠” alone is not a demonstrated advantage.
  Label an unverified differentiation as a hypothesis and preserve the strongest counterevidence.
- Resource fit: known time, cash, production skill, source access, repeatable material and comfort
  with face/voice exposure. Unknown constraints remain labeled assumptions. Check material platform,
  copyright, privacy or reuse constraints from accessible authoritative sources; do not assume that
  public content can be republished or that AI production is effortless/free. A learning log must
  not imply professional expertise, especially for health, legal or financial subjects.

If originals or live search are unavailable, apply SOURCE_BOUND / OFFLINE_REASONING as observed.
Disclose affected limits once. Use supplied evidence only to its scope; current demand, real channel
comparisons and plan/platform limits stay unverified where not inspected. Do not invent current
competitors to fill a table, call discovery snippets original-content inspection, or assign market
research as compulsory homework. Source-access recovery uses the existing lawful zero-extra-cost
ladder and specialist handoff; an unavailable source does not authorize payment or login.

### Conditional access/quality notice — creator-access-notice-01

Only when the user asks about a free AI environment or search/original-source access limits, or those
limits materially prevent the requested verification, add one short plain-language distinction:
“현재 이 대화에서 확인한 도구 범위만 알 수 있어요. 해당 무료 환경에서 전체 스킬·검색·원문·
파일 기능이 지원되는지와 결과 품질은 실제로 검증하지 않았고, 다른 모델과 같은 품질도
보장하지 않아요.” Adapt to the actual question; if no free environment is in scope, say “해당 환경”.
State only observed access; do not infer a provider/plan's support or quality from its price/name.
This is an unverified-support/quality notice, not a claim that FieldPilot never uses those tools.
It does not require login, paid upgrade, interviews, or user research to finish the bounded answer.

Apply explicit scope lock first. For names-only, wording-only or another narrow creative request with
no requested access/quality question, omit this notice entirely even if the host lacks web tools.
Do not force a market-access disclaimer into creator-05. Production ease, market evidence, host/plan
feature support and answer quality are separate claims: “AI 제작이 쉬운지 미검증” alone does not
close a requested environment-support/quality limit. Keep the existing evidence ceiling and QUESTION_GATE.

## Decision, HOLD and one next verification

Use `MARKET_DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.md` once evidence and alternatives are mapped. Recommend a current
concept only when evidence and feasibility separate it from plausible alternatives. Otherwise keep
DIRECTION_UNRESOLVED; no forced winner or required number of concepts. Deliver in plain language:
who this would help, the content promise/format, why now with actual sources, the meaningful difference,
resource fit, what to defer, what would reverse the choice, and one smallest next verification.

HOLD or narrow the investment when a decision-critical demand/competitor fact is blocked, production
cannot fit stated resources, access/rights make the format infeasible, or no supported distinction
survives the comparison. HOLD limits commitment; it does not end useful research or prove failure.
State the evidence or constraint change that would reopen the path. Do not claim guaranteed growth,
revenue, free-plan support or equal quality across models.

FieldPilot performs accessible research and prepares the exact comparison, outline or draft it can
prepare now. If the remaining uncertainty is production feasibility, the next discriminator may be a
private sample with actual time and source/rights checks; this tests production, not audience demand.
If audience response is the uncertainty and an authorized publication test is appropriate, reuse the
Execution Kit in `FREE_FIRST_AND_CHANNEL_ROUTING.md`: exact artifact/steps, lawful audience route,
observable behavior, accessible recording mechanism, context-derived checkpoint, exposure/measurement
health and positive/negative/ambiguous interpretation. Never publish, contact anyone or spend without
the existing authorization gate. No universal post count, duration or success percentage proves fit.

When response is absent, distinguish unobserved exposure, wrong audience, message/format, production
constraints and measurement failure before rejecting the concept. A private draft or completed plan
is not real audience evidence. Returning evidence updates only affected claims through Route D.
