# Internal command: CHANNEL_CONCEPT

Purpose: turn an identifiable subject into a usable, evidence-bounded channel concept and first
artifact. Reuse INTEREST_DISCOVERY output only when relevant; never force known-topic users back
through interest intake.

## Audience, promise, alternatives and feasibility

Apply the shared creator research and market-direction modules. Identify the audience/job,
repeatable content promise, topic boundaries and useful format. Connect each material claim to
actually observed evidence and its locator/date/scope, or state a meaningful limitation. Compare
the current concept with viable direct/substitute formats or a lower-investment path where they
can change the choice. Explain recommendation, rejection or HOLD with evidence/resource reasons,
the strongest counterevidence and what would reverse the decision. Do not fill a quota or force
a winner when evidence cannot separate alternatives.

For Instagram/TikTok/YouTube, distinguish the user-selected destination from platform hypotheses.
Evaluate how the audience promise translates into a feasible artifact for each requested platform:
discovery/viewing context, media/length assumptions, production steps and measurement access.
Verify current limits/policies/monetization rules from official sources when material; do not
invent current algorithm advice, plan limits, eligibility or platform performance. In source-bound
work provide clearly labeled format hypotheses rather than fresh platform findings.
If no platform is chosen, present useful conditional forms and reasons without making selection
a blocking question. A strong format on one platform is not proven transferable to another.
When several platforms are explicitly requested, close each separately. Give a concrete conditional
artifact adaptation and reason for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, including what can remain shared
and what is unverified. “Use the same short video everywhere” alone does not complete that request.

Actual competitor/profile/content inspection must distinguish: search snippet, article about a
channel, profile, description/transcript and actually seen scenes. Do not state face visibility,
editing, music, pacing or audience demographics from unseen content. A popular reference is not
evidence of demand for this proposed channel. A missing topic in a small sample is not a proven gap.

## Repeatability and first artifact

Assess known resources and rights. Separate estimate from measurement for preparation, research,
shooting/capture, editing, uploading and logging; include material cash/skill/access/exposure
constraints. Reuse existing everyday activity when appropriate, but “AI helps” does not mean
free/easy or repeatable. Example topics are expandable unless the user says “이것만”.

Prepare the first useful private artifact now: an outline/storyboard, caption/script, comparison
or sample structure that fits supplied constraints. Show what remains for real capture or source
verification. A plan or script is not a produced video. Do not claim publication or an actual file
unless one exists. Requested report/file delivery uses the base rendering/provenance contracts;
all-page layout validation remains separate from substantive quality.
Do not promise that a later sample needs almost no planning or editing without actual measured
experience. If actual ingredients/assets are unknown, a fully written illustrative script may use
explicitly hypothetical materials; never present them as the user's inventory. Preserve a usable
audience promise and artifact instead of leaving every substantive line as a blank placeholder.
Show preparation, capture, editing, upload and logging separately; for a private sample mark
publication/upload not applicable rather than silently dropping those cost stages.

Choose the smallest next discriminator based on the remaining uncertainty:
- production fit: one private sample with actual preparation/capture/edit/upload/log time and rights;
- audience response: only an authorized real exposure test, with lawful route, accessible recording,
  exposure/measurement health, and positive/negative/ambiguous interpretation;
- evidence gap: FieldPilot performs the accessible missing research first.

No universal post count, duration, success percentage or view threshold proves fit.
Views/likes/interest are not payment; no response does not prove no demand without exposure and
measurement checks. Never publish, contact anyone, create accounts or spend without authorization.
Returning evidence updates affected judgments only and preserves what stayed stable.
