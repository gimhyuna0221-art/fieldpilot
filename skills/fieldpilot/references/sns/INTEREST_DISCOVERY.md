# Internal command: INTEREST_DISCOVERY

Purpose: help a person discover plausible interests they may not have named yet, then choose what
is worth exploring for an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube channel. This is evidence-assisted exploration,
not a diagnosis of unconscious motives, personality, talent or a claim to know the user's mind.

## Restore signals and generate meaningful candidates

Use only user-supplied experience, recurring curiosity, saved/created material, everyday tasks,
things they compare or explain, difficulties they solve and actual comfort/resources. Existing
personal materials may be read only when relevant and authorized; do not mine unrelated private
history. Distinguish a quoted example from an exclusive limit: “자주 만드는 건 볶음밥/된장찌개”
does not mean those are the only possible dishes or topics.

Infer possible interest patterns as labeled hypotheses with the exact supporting user signal and
a plausible counterinterpretation. Distinguish enjoyment of watching, doing, explaining, comparing
and documenting; these have different production demands. An incidental mention alone is weak
evidence. Do not fabricate credentials, hobbies or identity. Never rank with invented interest scores.

Compare only meaningfully distinct candidates supported by known signals or explicitly hypothetical
exploration. No forced 2–3 niches, no fake market winner. Explain what fits, what is unsupported,
what would be hard to repeat and why a candidate is provisionally worth/not worth trying.
Use the shared creator module to research actual audience questions and existing formats where
retrieval is available, first in the provisional/explicit local market, then global references with
transfer limits. Audience demand cannot establish this individual's interest.

## Unknown interests

If the person says “몰라”, do not repeat the same questions, stall or pretend to have discovered
an interest. Use UNKNOWN for personal preference. Offer a concrete reversible exploration artifact
now, such as a short comparison draft, a process-note draft, or a beginner learning-log outline
around supplied everyday activity. Explain that these are probes for their reaction, not researched
market findings. Ask at most two missing personal facts only under the shared QUESTION_GATE.

If no subject or personal material exists, show a bounded menu of hypothetical experiences/formats
without invented audience demand and give a simple way to react by enjoyment and actual effort.
Do not require a personality test, public posting, new interviews, an account, a payment or a fixed
number of days/posts. A response of “몰라/없음” remains valid and narrows the claim ceiling.

## Handoff

Carry topic candidates, supporting personal signals, counterinterpretations, known/unknown resource
constraints and provisional choice to CHANNEL_CONCEPT when requested. Preserve the user's chosen
subject; do not change it merely because a generated alternative seems more popular.
Interest is still a hypothesis until the user's reaction or actual making experience supports it.
Do not call market demand, channel viability or monetization validated by this discovery alone.
