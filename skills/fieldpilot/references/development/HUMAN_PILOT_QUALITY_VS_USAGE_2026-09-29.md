# Human Pilot Feedback — Quality vs Usage — 2026-09-29

Source: owner-reported feedback from two external FieldPilot testers.

## Convergent feedback

### Positive
- FieldPilot found overlapping genres/categories the tester did not know existed.
- It brought representative games/products for each overlapping element and compared them.
- It searched very thoroughly for products with a similar feel.
- The optimized version appeared to use less AI quota/usage.

### Negative
- The reduced-usage version felt less rich and less detailed than the earlier version.
- It did not feel different enough from simply asking ChatGPT.
- The loss of richness was described as a subjective impression, not a measured quality score.

### Owner decision
- Stop optimizing primarily for usage reduction.
- Maximize result quality, discovery breadth and comparison depth even if usage increases.
- Keep only efficiency that removes pure duplication or wasted work.

## Claim ceiling

This is qualitative pilot feedback, not a controlled benchmark.

It does not establish:
- exact token savings;
- a measured quality percentage;
- willingness to pay;
- sales conversion;
- market-wide preference;
- guaranteed superiority over general-purpose AI.

## Product implication

Protect high-recall overlap/reference discovery as a signature behavior.

Do not trade away meaningful discovery or comparison detail merely to reduce usage.

The next external comparison should measure:
- products/references missed by general AI but found by FieldPilot;
- whether those discoveries are actually relevant;
- whether the findings change product/positioning decisions;
- whether the added depth is worth the extra usage.
